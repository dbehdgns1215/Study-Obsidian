## (3) 세부 사항
 >In the foreground of the picture, there is/are 사람/사물
> In the foreground of the picture, I can see 단/복수명사

> In the background of the picture, there is/are 사람/사물
> In the background of the picture, I can see 단/복수명사

> In the middle of the picture,  there is/are 사람/사물
> In the middle of the picture,  I can see 단/복수명사

> On the left side of the picture,  there is/are 사람/사물
> On the left side of the picture,  I can see 단/복수명사

> On the right side of the picture,  there is/are 사람/사물
> On the right side of the picture,  I can see 단/복수명사

> 마무리용 (1인 유형 일 때)
